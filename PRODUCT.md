# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary users are HR recruiters and IT recruiters evaluating Lowie Dave Dichoson for hiring opportunities. They are usually scanning quickly, comparing multiple candidates, and deciding whether this portfolio feels credible, memorable, and worth a deeper conversation. They need a fast read on technical capability, judgment, professionalism, and the sense that the work was personally made rather than assembled from a template.

## Product Purpose
This portfolio exists to turn a resume into a trust-building digital experience that helps Lowie stand out in hiring conversations. Its job is to create interview interest, strengthen recruiter trust, and increase the desire to hire by showing both the substance of the work and the care behind how it is presented. Success looks like a recruiter quickly understanding what Lowie builds, believing the craft and claims, and leaving with a strong impression that this is a complete and thoughtful engineer.

## Brand Personality
The brand personality is unique, personalized, and complete. The tone should feel confident, thoughtful, and human, with enough personality to feel distinctly handcrafted by Lowie. The emotional goal is not spectacle for its own sake, but trust, curiosity, and a strong sense of personal authorship.

## Anti-references
This should not look AI-generated, generic, or template-driven. Avoid designs that feel mass-produced, overly polished in a faceless way, or interchangeable with hundreds of portfolio clones. It should always read as something personally made by Lowie, with deliberate decisions and real point of view.

## Design Principles
1. Let the interface prove the engineer: the portfolio should demonstrate judgment, care, and technical fluency through the experience itself, not only through claims in the copy.
2. Optimize for recruiter confidence fast: the most important signals should be easy to scan, easy to trust, and easy to remember within a short visit.
3. Make personal authorship unmistakable: every major section should feel intentional and owned, so the work clearly reads as handcrafted rather than generated or templated.
4. Balance personality with professional clarity: the experience should feel distinctive and warm without making recruiters work harder to understand the story.
5. Show completeness, not just highlights: the portfolio should communicate that Lowie can carry work end to end, from technical execution to presentation, documentation, and polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA as a baseline. Support reduced motion preferences, strong contrast, and keyboard-first interaction across the experience. Important information should not rely on color alone, and the interface should remain legible, understandable, and usable for a broad range of visitors.
